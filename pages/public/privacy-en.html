Local records

PayGuard stores subscriptions, purchases, warranties, return deadlines, notes, reminder preferences, attachments and manually added family names and roles locally. App use does not require account creation.

How records are used

Records support tracking, summaries, widgets and reminders. Receipts, screenshots, scans and PDFs may be analyzed on the device to suggest information for a record. Review imported suggestions yourself.

Permissions and sharing

Notifications are optional. Records and attachments remain local unless you choose to export or share them. PayGuard does not sell personal data or share it for advertising or marketing.

Delete your data

Delete individual entries or use Delete all local data in Settings to remove subscriptions, purchases, household members and pending reminders.

Apple purchases

Apple processes any in-app purchases. Payment card information is handled by Apple, not this website.

Age and updates

PayGuard is not directed to children under 13. Changes to this policy will be published on this page.

This website

This static site has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request information to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact and your choices

Voluntary support emails are processed to answer your request; app records are not attached automatically. You can email requests to access, correct or delete personal information in support messages. Messages are kept as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.
